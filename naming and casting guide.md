# Custom 2 — easier names + character casting

Two things here: **table-friendly names** for the roles that are awkward to call out loud,
and **three casting options per role** drawn from your franchise list.

Every suggestion avoids the 39 Demon Slayer names already printed on your deck.
Franchises used: DBZ · AOT · Bleach · Spy x Family · Chainsaw Man · DC · Marvel · GoT ·
Breaking Bad · Witcher · Peaky Blinders · The Boys · Pirates · Invincible · X-Men ·
Mortal Kombat · Ben 10 · Resident Evil · God of War.

---

## Part 1 — Renames

### The problem you actually have

**Six cards in the pool have "Wolf" in the name:** Wolf-Hound, Wolf Man, Wolf Cub,
Dream Wolf, Lone Wolf, White Wolf — plus Fruit Brute makes seven wolf-type cards.
When the moderator says *"Wolf…"* at night, the whole table twitches. The renames below
fix that first, and readability second.

### Strongly recommended

| Current | New name | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Wolf Man** | **Sheep** | A wolf in sheep's clothing — reads as a villager. Instantly memorable, kills the "Wolf-" collision |
| **Dream Wolf** | **Sleeper** | Exactly what it does: dormant until a wolf dies |
| **Wolf Cub** | **Cub** | Same card, one less "Wolf" |
| **Lone Wolf** | **Outcast** | Removes the clash with White Wolf, who is also a lone wolf |
| **Fruit Brute** | **Famine** | "Fruit Brute" is a cereal-mascot joke and doesn't read as a wolf at all. *Famine* says "the wolf who cannot eat" |
| **Old Man** | **Doomed** | **"Old Man" and "Elder" will be confused every single game.** Also describes the card: dies on a fixed night |
| **Doppelgänger** | **Mimic** | Two syllables, no umlaut, no spelling argument |
| **Chupacabra** | **Reaper** | Four syllables and nobody says it the same way twice |
| **Spellcaster** | **Silencer** | Says what it does. "Spellcaster" suggests magic generally |

### Worth doing

| Current | New name | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Nostradamus** | **Gambler** | He bets on a winner. Short, and no confusion with the Seer |
| **Leprechaun** | **Trickster** | Easier to say and spell; misdirection is the whole card |
| **Hoodlum** | **Hitman** | He names two targets and wins if both die. "Hoodlum" says nothing |
| **Tough Guy** | **Bruiser** | One word, unmistakable |
| **Insomniac** | **Watcher** | Shorter. *(Only if you actually play it — see the review)* |

### Keep as-is

**Ghost** · **Lycan** · **Mayor** · **Bloody Mary** · **Sasquatch** · **Bogeyman** ·
**Diseased** — all short, distinct, and already evocative.

### After the renames, your wolf line-up reads

> Muzan · Kokushibo · Douma · Nakime · Gyutaro · Rui · Enmu · **Sheep** · **Cub** ·
> **Sleeper** · **Outcast** · **Famine**

No two sound alike. That is the whole point.

## Part 2 — Casting

Three options each. The **first** is my pick.

### Village

**Ghost** — *dies at setup, then writes one-letter clues*
1. **Ned Stark** (GoT) — dies first, and the whole story is people decoding what he left behind
2. **Uncle Ben** (Marvel) — dead from the start, still guiding every decision
3. **Deadman / Boston Brand** (DC) — an actual ghost who communicates with the living

**Watcher** *(Insomniac)* — *learns a neighbour woke*
1. **Anya Forger** (Spy x Family) — hears what the people next to her are thinking. Perfect
2. **Uatu the Watcher** (Marvel) — observes, never intervenes
3. **Oracle / Barbara Gordon** (DC) — the one who sees all the movement

**Lycan** — *villager who reads as a werewolf*
1. **Beast / Hank McCoy** (X-Men) — looks like a monster, is the gentlest man in the room
2. **Geralt of Rivia** (Witcher) — called a mutant and a monster everywhere he goes
3. **Bruce Banner** (Marvel) — the village will always suspect him

**Trickster** *(Leprechaun)* — *redirects the wolf attack to a neighbour*
1. **Loki** (Marvel) — misdirection is the entire character
2. **Jack Sparrow** (Pirates) — survives by making danger land on somebody else
3. **Zatanna** (DC) — literal misdirection magic

**Mayor** — *double vote if revealed*
1. **Tommy Shelby** (Peaky Blinders) — actually becomes an MP and commands the room
2. **Tyrion Lannister** (GoT) — Hand of the King; his word carries twice the weight
3. **Gus Fring** (Breaking Bad) — the respected civic pillar everyone defers to

**Doomed** *(Old Man)* — *dies on a fixed night*
1. **Maester Aemon** (GoT) — old, wise, and his time simply runs out
2. **Odin** (Marvel) — knows the date of his own death and meets it calmly
3. **Vesemir** (Witcher) — the old mentor whose death is written in

**Silencer** *(Spellcaster)* — *mutes one player for the next day*
1. **Yennefer** (Witcher) — sorceress, and silencing a rival is very much her
2. **Quan Chi** (Mortal Kombat) — sorcerer who binds and controls
3. **Doctor Strange** (Marvel) — a gesture and you cannot speak

**Bruiser** *(Tough Guy)* — *survives one extra day*
1. **Luke Cage** (Marvel) — unbreakable skin, walks it off
2. **Sandor Clegane / The Hound** (GoT) — takes appalling punishment and keeps going
3. **The Immortal** (Invincible) — dying is a temporary inconvenience

**Bloody Mary** — *kills from beyond the grave*
1. **Scorpion** (Mortal Kombat) — died, came back, hunts the ones responsible. Exact match
2. **Ghost Rider** (Marvel) — the Spirit of Vengeance
3. **The Spectre** (DC) — vengeance itself, wearing a dead man

### Might change to wolf

**Diseased** — *villager who turns wolf when attacked*
1. **Eren Yeager** (AOT) — gets **eaten** and becomes the monster. The card, literally
2. **Jill Valentine** (Resident Evil) — infected and turned against her own side
3. **Morbius** (Marvel) — a man whose cure makes him the predator

**Mimic** *(Doppelgänger)* — *takes a dead player's role*
1. **Shang Tsung** (Mortal Kombat) — takes your soul **when you die** and wears your form
2. **Mystique** (X-Men) — becomes anyone, perfectly
3. **Martian Manhunter** (DC) — shapeshifter who can be anybody at the table

**Sasquatch** — *turns wolf after a day with no lynch*
1. **William Birkin** (Resident Evil) — the colleague who becomes the thing in the basement
2. **Denji** (Chainsaw Man) — ordinary kid, devil underneath, one trigger away
3. **Kevin Levin** (Ben 10) — starts as one of you, ends as something else

### Wolves

**Sheep** *(Wolf Man)* — *a wolf who reads as a villager*
1. **Sōsuke Aizen** (Bleach) — the kind captain everybody trusted. The definitive version
2. **Walter White** (Breaking Bad) — a harmless teacher right up until he isn't
3. **Petyr Baelish / Littlefinger** (GoT) — the helpful friend holding the knife

**Cub** *(Wolf Cub)* — *its death gives the pack two kills*
1. **Power** (Chainsaw Man) — feral, childlike, and killing her enrages everything around her
2. **Rickon Stark** (GoT) — the child whose death sets the field on fire
3. **Oliver Grayson** (Invincible) — small, young, and not remotely harmless

**Sleeper** *(Dream Wolf)* — *dormant until a wolf dies*
1. **Bucky Barnes / Winter Soldier** (Marvel) — a sleeper agent, activated on a trigger
2. **Reiner Braun** (AOT) — lived among them for years before waking up
3. **Android 17** (DBZ) — sealed and dormant until someone opens the box

**Outcast** *(Lone Wolf)* — *only wins as the last wolf standing*
1. **Omni-Man / Nolan** (Invincible) — turns on his own side without hesitating
2. **Kratos** (God of War) — kills his entire pantheon and stands alone
3. **Sabretooth** (X-Men) — has no team, only rivals

**Famine** *(Fruit Brute)* — *as last wolf, cannot feed*
1. **Emiel Regis** (Witcher) — a vampire who **abstains from blood by choice**. Uncanny fit
2. **Coyote Starrk** (Bleach) — the wolf-themed Espada whose whole curse is being alone
3. **Zeke Yeager** (AOT) — the monster who'd rather end it by argument than by eating

### Loners

**Bogeyman** — *kills when the wolves deadlock; wins when every night role is dead*
1. **The Night King** (GoT) — wants everything that matters dead, and is patient about it
2. **Darkness Devil** (Chainsaw Man) — arrives when things stall and the room simply ends
3. **Black Noir** (The Boys) — silent, steps in when others can't finish the job

**Reaper** *(Chupacabra)* — *nightly kill; wins as sole survivor*
1. **Makima** (Chainsaw Man) — uses everyone, kills everyone, intends to be what's left
2. **Deathstroke** (DC) — takes contracts from nobody's side but his own
3. **Homelander** (The Boys) — wants a world with only himself in it

**Hitman** *(Hoodlum)* — *names two targets night 1*
1. **Jaqen H'ghar** (GoT) — *"a girl names names."* Made for this card
2. **Mike Ehrmantraut** (Breaking Bad) — professional, unhurried, two names on the list
3. **Yor Forger** (Spy x Family) — the sweetest person at the table has a kill list

**Gambler** *(Nostradamus)* — *predicts the winning team*
1. **Melisandre** (GoT) — reads the flames, picks a champion, and is sometimes catastrophically wrong
2. **Gambit** (X-Men) — the name does the work
3. **Mimir** (God of War) — the head that knows how everything ends
